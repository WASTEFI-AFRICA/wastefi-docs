```markdown
2. Material verification and quality grading
```

### Template 2: Technology Innovation Grant

```markdown
# Innovation Grant Application: RecycleGraph Open Standard

## Project Title
RecycleGraph: Open Standard for Material Identification in Circular Economy

## Innovation Description

### Problem
Current recycling systems lack:
- Universal material identification standards
- Cross-platform interoperability
- Verifiable tracking and transparency
- Efficient EPR compliance mechanisms

### Innovation
RecycleGraph is an open standard enabling:
- Universal material identification (like ISBN for books)
- Digital product passports on blockchain
- Cross-platform data exchange
- Cryptographic verification
- AI-powered quality grading

### Technology Stack
- Blockchain: Stellar (for immutability)
- Standards: JSON Schema, QR/NFC
- APIs: RESTful, GraphQL
- AI/ML: Material recognition, quality grading
- Mobile: PWA with offline-first design

## Market Opportunity

**Total Addressable Market:**
- Global recycling industry: $400B
- Waste management software: $3B
- Carbon credit market: $800B (projected)

**Target Users:**
- Waste collectors: 20M globally
- Recycling facilities: 50,000+
- Manufacturers: 100,000+ (EPR compliance)
- Governments: Universal standard adoption

## Competitive Advantage

**Unique Value Propositions:**
1. **Open Standard:** Free to implement, no lock-in
2. **Blockchain-Agnostic:** Works with any ledger
3. **Offline-First:** Works in low-connectivity areas
4. **Interoperable:** Cross-platform by design
5. **Privacy-Preserving:** Minimal data collection
```

### Case Study Example

```markdown
# Case Study: Kibera Collection Point

## Background
- **Location:** Kibera, Nairobi's largest informal settlement
- **Population:** 200,000+ residents
- **Challenge:** Poor waste management, no recycling infrastructure
- **Start Date:** March 2024

## Implementation

### Setup (Month 1)
- Partnered with local community organization
- Trained 3 operators
- Set up sorting facility (50m²)
- Onboarded 35 collectors

### Operations (Months 2-6)
- Average 120 transactions/month
- 15 tons waste processed/month
- 42 active collectors

### Environmental
- **90 tons** waste diverted (6 months)

- **68% recycling rate** (vs 12% baseline)

### Social
- **42 collectors** earning regular income

### Challenge: Low Initial Participation
**Solution:** Peer ambassadors, demonstration days
```

## Tips for Success

### Grant Writing

1. **Tell a Story:** Use narratives, not just numbers
2. **Be Specific:** Concrete goals and metrics
3. **Show Evidence:** Data, case studies, research
4. **Demonstrate Capacity:** Team experience, track record
5. **Budget Realistically:** Detailed, justified costs
6. **Plan Sustainability:** Path beyond grant funding

### Impact Reporting

1. **Be Transparent:** Report challenges, not just successes
2. **Use Visuals:** Photos, charts, infographics
3. **Include Stories:** Humanize the data
4. **Compare to Baseline:** Show change over time
5. **Report Regularly:** Build trust through consistency
6. **Verify Independently:** Third-party validation adds credibility
