# Vision & Mission

## Project Vision

**Financial Inclusion Through Waste Collection - Powered by Open Material Standards**

### Phased Approach

**Phase 1 (MVP): WasteFi Africa**
- Mobile-first waste banking for emerging markets
- Focus on informal waste collectors
- Integration with mobile money providers
- Stellar blockchain payments

**Phase 2 (Standards Layer): RecycleGraph Protocol**
- Open material identification standards
- Cross-chain interoperability
- Digital product passport API
- Verification protocol

**End Goal: Global Waste-to-Value Platform**
- Open standards that anyone can implement
- Verifiable impact data monetization
- Carbon credits and EPR compliance
- Financial inclusion at scale

## The Problem

Informal waste collectors in emerging markets:
- Handle 50-80% of waste in developing countries
- Earn poverty-level wages ($1-3 per day)
- Lack access to formal financial systems
- Have no proof of environmental impact
- Cannot access carbon credit markets

## Our Solution

WasteFi creates a bridge between:
- Informal waste collectors
- Formal financial systems
- Environmental impact markets
- Global recycling standards

Through:
- Instant mobile payments
- Blockchain transparency
- Open material standards
- Verifiable impact data

## Mission Statement

To empower waste collectors worldwide by:
1. Providing fair, instant payment for collected materials
2. Creating verifiable records of environmental impact
3. Enabling access to carbon credit and compliance markets
4. Building open standards that scale globally

## Core Values

- **Inclusion First** - Design for those excluded from traditional systems
- **Transparency** - Open source, open standards, open data
- **Sustainability** - Environmental and economic sustainability together
- **Empowerment** - Give collectors tools to build better futures
- **Interoperability** - Build standards anyone can implement

## Impact Goals

By 2030, we aim to:
- Serve 1 million+ waste collectors
- Process 1 million+ tons of verified recycled materials
- Generate $100 million+ in income for collectors
- Create standardized material passports globally
