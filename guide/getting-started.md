# Getting Started

Welcome to WasteFi! This guide will help you understand the platform and get started quickly.

## What is WasteFi?

WasteFi is a financial inclusion platform that connects waste collectors in emerging markets with blockchain-based payments and open material standards.

### Key Features

- **Mobile Waste Banking** - Collectors can deposit waste and receive instant payments
- **Stellar Blockchain** - Fast, low-cost transactions with global reach
- **Mobile Money Integration** - Cash out to M-Pesa, MTN Money, and other providers
- **Material Standards** - Uses RecycleGraph protocol for standardized tracking
- **Impact Verification** - Verifiable environmental data for carbon credits

## Platform Architecture

WasteFi consists of four main components:

1. **Smart Contracts** - Stellar Soroban contracts for payments and verification
2. **Backend API** - Business logic, integrations, and data management
3. **Frontend Apps** - Mobile PWA for collectors and admin dashboard
4. **Documentation** - Guides, standards, and developer resources (you are here!)

## Who Should Use This Documentation?

- **Waste Collectors** - Learn how to use the mobile app and get paid
- **Collection Point Operators** - Manage your collection center
- **Developers** - Integrate WasteFi or build on the platform
- **Organizations** - Understand the technology and impact potential
