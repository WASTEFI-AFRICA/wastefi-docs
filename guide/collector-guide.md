# Collector Guide

## Welcome to WasteFi!

This guide will help you start earning money by collecting waste materials. WasteFi pays you fairly and instantly for the materials you collect.

## Getting Started

### What You Need

1. **A mobile phone** - Smartphone or basic phone with SMS
2. **Phone number** - To register and receive payments
3. **Materials to collect** - Plastic bottles, cans, paper, etc.

### How It Works

```
1. Collect Materials → 2. Bring to Collection Point → 3. Get Paid Instantly
```

---

## Step 1: Register

### Using a Smartphone

1. Open your mobile browser
2. Go to **app.wastefi.org**
3. Tap **"Register"**
4. Enter your phone number (e.g., +254712345678)
5. Enter your name
6. Create a 4-digit PIN (remember this!)
7. You'll receive an SMS with a code
8. Enter the code to verify your phone
9. Done! You're registered

### Using a Basic Phone (SMS)

1. Send **JOIN** to **40404**
2. Reply with your name when asked
3. Create a 4-digit PIN
4. You'll receive a confirmation SMS

**Important:** Keep your PIN safe! You'll need it to login and receive payments.

---

## Step 2: Find a Collection Point

### Using the App

1. Open the WasteFi app
2. Tap **"Find Collection Point"**
3. See nearby points on the map
4. Tap a point to see:
   - Address and directions
   - Opening hours
   - Accepted materials
   - Current prices

### Using SMS

Send **FIND** to **40404** to get the nearest collection point address.

---

## Step 3: Collect Materials

### What Materials Can You Collect?

| Material | Examples | Price Range |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **PET Plastic** | Water bottles, soda bottles | $0.25-0.35/kg |
| **HDPE Plastic** | Milk jugs, detergent bottles | $0.20-0.30/kg |
| **Aluminum** | Soda cans, food cans | $0.60-0.90/kg |
| **Cardboard** | Boxes, packaging | $0.10-0.15/kg |
| **Glass** | Bottles | $0.05-0.10/kg |

*Prices vary by location and quality*

### Quality Grades

Get better prices with higher quality!

**Grade A (Best) - +20% bonus**
- Clean and dry
- Labels removed
- No contamination
- Sorted by type

**Grade B (Good) - Standard price**
- Clean
- Some labels OK
- Sorted by type

**Grade C (Fair) - -20% penalty**
- Dirty or contaminated
- Mixed materials
- Damaged

### Collection Tips

✅ **DO:**
- Remove labels when possible
- Rinse bottles and cans
- Flatten cardboard boxes
- Sort by material type
- Keep materials dry

❌ **DON'T:**
- Mix food waste with recyclables
- Bring hazardous materials
- Collect damaged/broken items
- Bring wet or moldy materials

---

## Step 4: Deposit Materials

### At the Collection Point

1. **Arrive during opening hours**
2. **Tell the operator your phone number**
3. **Show your materials**
4. The operator will:
   - Weigh your materials
   - Check the quality (A, B, or C)
   - Calculate your payment
5. **Check the amount on your phone**
6. **Approve the transaction with your PIN**
7. **Payment received instantly!**

### Payment Example

You bring 5.5 kg of clean PET bottles (Grade A):

```
Material: PET Plastic Bottles
Weight: 5.5 kg
Quality: A (1.2x bonus)
Base price: $0.30/kg
Location bonus: +5%

Calculation:
5.5 kg × $0.30 × 1.2 × 1.05 = $2.08

Platform fee (2%): -$0.04
You receive: $2.04
```

You'll also save **13.75 kg of CO2** from entering landfills!

## Earning More

### Tips to Maximize Earnings

1. **Aim for Grade A quality**
   - Clean materials = 20% more money
   - Takes only a few minutes

2. **Collect valuable materials**
   - Aluminum pays 3x more than plastic
   - Focus on high-value items

3. **Work consistently**
   - Collect daily for steady income
   - Build relationships with collection points

4. **Track prices**
   - Prices change based on market
   - Check app for current rates

5. **Collect in bulk**
   - Save time on trips
   - Negotiate better rates for large amounts

### Bonus Opportunities

**Carbon Credits:**
- Earn extra from environmental impact
- Paid quarterly based on verified CO2 savings
- Usually $0.02-0.05 per kg additional

**Referral Bonus:**
- Refer friends to WasteFi
- Get $2 bonus per referral
- They get $2 signup bonus too!

## Common Questions

### How much can I earn?

It depends on how much you collect:

**Part-time (5-10 kg/day):**
- $1.50-3.00 per day
- $45-90 per month

**Full-time (20-30 kg/day):**
- $6-9 per day
- $180-270 per month

**Plus carbon credit bonuses!**

### When do I get paid?

**Instantly!** Payment is processed within 1-2 minutes of deposit.

### What if I disagree with the weight or price?

1. Check the amount before approving
2. If you disagree, **don't approve** the transaction
3. Ask the operator to reweigh
4. If still disagreeing, you can:
   - Refuse the transaction
   - Try another collection point
   - File a dispute in the app

### What if payment doesn't arrive?

1. Wait 5 minutes (sometimes there's a delay)
2. Check your mobile money account
3. Contact collection point operator
4. Use app to report the issue
5. Or send **HELP** to **40404**

### Can I collect materials anywhere?

Yes, but you must deposit at a registered WasteFi collection point to get paid through the platform.

### Is there a minimum or maximum amount?

**Minimum:** Usually 0.1 kg (100 grams)
**Maximum:** Varies by collection point, usually 100 kg per visit

### What if I forget my PIN?

1. Open the app
2. Tap "Forgot PIN?"
3. Enter your phone number
4. You'll receive an SMS code
5. Enter code and create new PIN

**Or send RESET to 40404**

## Safety Tips

### Personal Safety

- Work during daylight hours
- Tell someone where you're going
- Wear gloves when handling waste
- Avoid hazardous materials
- Stay hydrated

### Material Safety

**Never collect:**
- Medical waste (syringes, bandages)
- Chemical containers
- Batteries (except at special centers)
- Electronic waste with batteries
- Broken glass without protection

### Health Tips

- Wash hands frequently
- Don't eat while working
- Wear closed shoes
- Use gloves and face mask if available
- Seek medical attention for cuts

## Troubleshooting

### App Problems

**Can't log in:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify phone number is correct
3. Try "Forgot PIN?" if needed
4. Reinstall app if still failing

**App is slow:**
- Close other apps
- Check internet connection
- Clear app cache
- Restart phone

### Payment Problems

**Payment not received:**
1. Wait 5 minutes
2. Check mobile money balance
3. Contact collection point
4. Report in app

**Wrong amount paid:**
1. Check transaction details in app
2. File a dispute if incorrect
3. Provide photos of materials if you have them
