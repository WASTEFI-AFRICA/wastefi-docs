# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### What is WasteFi?

WasteFi is a platform that pays you instantly for collecting waste materials. We use blockchain technology to ensure fair prices and transparent payments.

### Who can use WasteFi?

Anyone with a mobile phone! Collectors, recycling businesses, and organizations can all use WasteFi.

### Is WasteFi free to use?

Yes! Registration and using the platform is completely free. We only take a small 2% fee from transactions to cover operating costs.

### Which countries is WasteFi available in?

Currently: Kenya, Ghana, and Nigeria. We're expanding to more African countries soon!

## Registration & Account

### How do I register?

**Smartphone:** Go to app.wastefi.org and follow the prompts  
**Basic phone:** Send "JOIN" to 40404

### What information do I need to provide?

- Phone number
- Name
- 4-digit PIN (you create this)

That's it! No email or ID required.

### I forgot my PIN. What do I do?

1. In app: Tap "Forgot PIN?"
2. Via SMS: Send "RESET" to 40404
3. Follow instructions to create a new PIN

### Can I change my phone number?

Yes! Contact support at support@wastefi.org or call our helpline. We'll help you update your account.

### Can I have multiple accounts?

No, one account per phone number. This ensures fair payment distribution and prevents fraud.

## Materials & Pricing

### What materials can I collect?

- **Plastics:** PET, HDPE, LDPE bottles and containers
- **Metals:** Aluminum cans, steel cans
- **Paper:** Cardboard, newspapers, office paper
- **Glass:** Bottles and containers

### How are prices determined?

Prices are based on:
- Current market rates
- Material type and quality
- Your location
- Supply and demand

Prices are updated daily and are always transparent.

### Why do prices change?

Material prices fluctuate based on global commodity markets, just like oil or gold. We pass fair market prices to you.

### What are quality grades?

- **Grade A (+20%):** Clean, sorted, labels removed
- **Grade B (standard):** Clean and sorted
- **Grade C (-20%):** Dirty or mixed materials

### Can I negotiate prices?

Prices are set by market rates and are the same for everyone. This ensures fairness and transparency.

## Payments

### How quickly do I get paid?

Instantly! Payments are processed within 1-2 minutes of your deposit being approved.

### How do I receive payment?

Through mobile money:
- **Kenya:** M-Pesa
- **Ghana:** MTN Mobile Money or Vodafone Cash
- **Nigeria:** MTN Money, Airtel Money

### Is there a minimum payout?

No minimum! Even 100 grams (0.1 kg) gets paid.

### Are there any fees?

- **Platform fee:** 2% of transaction value
- **Blockchain fee:** ~$0.00001 (negligible)
- **Mobile money fees:** Charged by your provider (usually free for receiving)

### What if payment doesn't arrive?

1. Wait 5 minutes
2. Check your mobile money balance
3. Contact the collection point
4. Report via app or SMS: "HELP" to 40404

### Can I cash out my balance?

Your payments go directly to mobile money, so you can withdraw from any mobile money agent immediately.

## Environmental Impact

### How is CO2 saved calculated?

Based on standard recycling impact factors:
- CO2 prevented from landfills
- Energy saved in production
- Resources conserved

Our calculations are verified by third-party auditors.

### What are carbon credits?

Certificates representing CO2 you've prevented from entering the atmosphere. Companies buy these to offset their emissions.

### How do I earn from carbon credits?

- You earn them automatically through collecting
- We aggregate and sell credits quarterly
- You get 70% of the revenue (we keep 30%)
- Payments made to your account

### When are carbon credit payments made?

Every quarter (January, April, July, October) after verification.

## Safety & Security

### Is my money safe?

Yes! Payments are processed on Stellar blockchain, which is secure and transparent. We don't hold your funds.

### Is my personal information protected?

We only collect essential information (phone, name) and never share it without permission. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

### What if someone steals my phone?

1. Contact your mobile money provider to freeze account
2. Email support@wastefi.org to suspend your WasteFi account
3. Create new account with new number

### How do I protect my account?

- Keep PIN secret
- Don't share OTP codes
- Log out on shared devices
- Report suspicious activity immediately

## Disputes

### What if I disagree with the weight?

1. Don't approve the transaction
2. Ask operator to reweigh
3. If still disagreeing, take materials to another point
4. File dispute in app if you already approved

### How do I file a dispute?

**In app:**
1. Go to transaction history
2. Tap the transaction
3. Tap "Dispute"
4. Provide details and photos if possible

**Via SMS:** Send "DISPUTE [transaction ID]" to 40404

### How long does dispute resolution take?

Usually 2-3 business days. You'll receive updates via SMS.

### What if my dispute is rejected?

You'll receive an explanation. If you still disagree, you can escalate to our appeals team.

## Earnings & Income

### How much can I realistically earn?

**Part-time (5-10 kg/day):** $45-90/month  
**Full-time (20-30 kg/day):** $180-270/month  
**Plus carbon credit bonuses!**

### Can I make this my full-time job?

Yes! Many collectors earn enough to support themselves and their families. Success depends on:

### Are there bonuses?

Yes!

- **Referral bonus:** $2 per friend you refer
- **Milestone rewards:** For reaching collection goals

### Do I pay taxes on earnings?

Tax rules vary by country. WasteFi provides transaction records for tax reporting. Consult local tax authorities.
